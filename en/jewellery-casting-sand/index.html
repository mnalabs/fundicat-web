    Casting sand for jewellery and artistic casting

    PetroBond is a fine, oil-bonded sand that needs no water and keeps its shape after every pour. It is a popular choice among jewellers, sculptors and small workshops for making precise sand moulds, with no lost-wax step needed for simple pieces.

    What it is for

    Rings, pendants, medals, small sculptures and parts in aluminium, bronze, brass and silver. It is also handy for quick prototypes before moving on to a final mould.

    How you work with it

    You sieve it, pack it around the pattern inside a flask (the mould box), cut the pouring channels and pour the molten metal. The whole process is explained step by step in our sand casting guide.

    Reusable

    After each pour, sieve it and pack it again. Keep it covered, away from damp and dust, and break up any lumps before making a new mould.

    Safety

    Always work with safety glasses, gloves and good ventilation. Molten metal and sand must be dry and kept away from liquids.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need to add water to PetroBond?

    No. It is an oil-bonded sand: use it as it comes, sieved and well packed.

    How many times can it be reused?

    Many times, as long as it stays clean and keeps the right texture. Sieve it and pack it again after each pour.

    Which metals can be cast in PetroBond moulds?

    Typically gold, silver, bronze, brass and aluminium, along with other low-melting alloys.

    How much sand do I need?

    It depends on your flask. A 5 kg bag is enough for small flasks; larger boxes or several moulds will need more.
